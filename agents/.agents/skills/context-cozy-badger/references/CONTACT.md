## Routing instructions

- Use the company website for general company links and the code organization
  for public repositories.
- Use the general-inquiry email or contact page for business inquiries.
- For bugs, feature requests, contributions, and security reports, follow the
  target repository's instructions and designated channels. Do not publish
  vulnerability details in a public issue unless its policy permits this.
- Read current official pages before publishing legal or contact details.
  Link to them rather than copying addresses, legal identifiers, or policies
  into unrelated project files.
- Do not invent phone numbers, social accounts, role-specific email addresses,
  response-time commitments, or support guarantees.
- These destinations provide context, not permission to contact anyone,
  submit forms, or post on the company's behalf.
